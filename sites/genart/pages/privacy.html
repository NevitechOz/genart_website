This privacy notice describes how and why Nevitech ("we," "us," or "our") might collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your information when you use our mobile apps, our websites, including genartstudio.org, and any other related services ("Services"). If you do not agree with this notice, please do not use our Services. Questions? Contact us at nevitechoz@gmail.com.

This page has two parts: the general terms that apply to every Nevitech app, and a section for each app describing its specific features and data practices. Where an app section differs from the general part, the app section applies to that app.

Information We Process

Depending on the app and the features you use, we may process the following:

  • Information you provide, such as account details, content you create or submit, and messages you send us.
  • Sign-in information. If you sign in with a third-party account such as Google, we receive the details that provider shares with us under its own privacy policy, such as your email address, display name and avatar. You can control what is shared through your Google activity controls.
  • Purchase information. Apple or Google handles checkout. We and our purchase provider receive purchase and customer identifiers and entitlement information, not your payment-card details.
  • Device and usage information collected by analytics, crash-reporting and advertising services, which can include device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, interaction data and diagnostics.

We do not intentionally process sensitive personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain and improve our apps and Services.
  • To operate your account, purchases and credits.
  • To respond to your enquiries and support requests.
  • To keep our Services secure and to prevent fraud and abuse, in line with our legitimate interest in protecting our users and Services.
  • To measure performance and show advertising in apps that carry ads.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to external parties.

Service providers – We use providers to run our apps, for example Firebase and Google services for authentication, analytics, crash reporting and advertising, RevenueCat for purchases, and AI providers for apps with AI features. They process data on our behalf or under their own terms. Each app section lists the providers that app uses.

Compliance with laws – We may disclose your personal data to courts, law enforcement agencies and governmental authorities (i) to comply with our legal obligations, (ii) to respond to legal process and to claims asserted against us, (iii) to respond to verified requests relating to a criminal investigation or alleged or suspected illegal activity, (iv) to enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements with users, or (v) to protect the rights, property or personal safety of Nevitech, its employees and members of the public.

Features you choose to use – If you share content or connect an app to other apps or social networks, the data you share is subject to those services' terms.

Information Security

We maintain appropriate technical, administrative and physical safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, use or disclosure of personal data we collect or store, and we regularly monitor our systems for possible vulnerabilities and attacks. However, it is not possible to guarantee the security of data transmitted over the Internet. You use our Services and provide personal data at your own discretion and risk.

Your Choices, Access and Deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to delete your account, by emailing nevitechoz@gmail.com with the app name, or through the steps on our Contact Us page. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account. Uninstalling an app does not necessarily remove backend, store or provider records. Each app section explains what its deletion covers. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or access tokens.

On iOS and Android you can also reset your advertising identifier and manage tracking and personalised-ads permissions in your device settings. Your rights depend on where you live.

GenArt AI Studio

GenArt AI Studio generates images from text prompts and lets you post and share them.

Information we collect

When you register, interact with the app or website, or contact us, we may collect your email address, username and password, and the prompts, images and comments you submit. You can sign up and log in with your Google account; Google then shares your Google email address and avatar (optional) with us, under Google's privacy policy. If you signed up with Google by mistake, you can delete your account from the Account page in Settings at any time, or contact us.

How we use it

  • To operate and maintain your account and give you access to GenArt AI Studio. Your email address and password identify you when you log in.
  • To identify you as the creator of the images you upload, the comments you post and the other contributions you make.
  • To respond to any comment or enquiry you submit.
  • To maintain a safe and secure environment.

How it is shared

Through your profile – Personal data you choose to disclose in your public profile, and images you post publicly, can be viewed and commented on by other users.

Third-party applications – If you connect your account to other apps or social networks such as Facebook and X, you may share certain personal data with them. Content you share appears subject to their terms and may be viewed by the public or their followers.

Advertising – GenArt AI Studio shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, usage data, approximate location and diagnostics, as described in our Terms of Service.

GenWord

GenWord is a vocabulary-learning app for Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Your learning selections, saved review words, study progress, quiz history and display settings are stored on your device. We do not promise cloud backup or synchronization; availability depends on the app version.

Optional online features and service providers

  • Account sign-in is optional; you can study without an account. If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication processes the information needed to authenticate you (such as your Google email address and display name), and we can associate your account identifier with your purchase and credit records.
  • Purchases – Apple or Google handles checkout. RevenueCat processes purchase and customer identifiers, entitlement information and GCoin balances used by the app. Do not send payment-card details to Nevitech support.
  • AI word generation sends your request to our backend and to the configured AI provider, and saves generated collections to your signed-in account. Feedback you submit is saved with your account identifier. Do not include sensitive personal information in either feature.
  • Analytics, crash reporting and advertising – the app can use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics and Google AdMob. These can collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, interaction data and diagnostics. Ads are removed with GenWord Pro.
  • Google Drive backup is optional and user-initiated. It uses the limited drive.file scope, so the app can only access files it creates in your own Drive.

Deleting your GenWord data

You can request account deletion in the app under Settings → Account Deletion, which cleans up your server-side account after a seven-day grace period, or by emailing us with "GenWord privacy request". Deletion requires fresh sign-in verification. It does not erase local learning data on your device or backups in your own Google Drive.

After an account is deleted we keep keyed digests of sign-in identifiers to prevent repeated welcome-credit claims; these do not contain raw provider identifiers. Feedback records become eligible for automatic deletion after 30 days; deletion is asynchronous. Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat follow their own retention.
