Contact Us - Nevitech

We're here to help with any question or request about Nevitech apps and websites. Email nevitechoz@gmail.com and put the app name in the subject so we can route it. We aim to respond within 7-10 business days. This is a response target, not a promise that every request will be completed in that time.

This page covers all Nevitech apps. For a new or unlisted app, include its exact name and store link in your message.

Account Deletion Request

To delete your account and associated personal data, email us with the subject "Account Deletion Request - [app name]" and include:

  • The app name.
  • The email address or identifier you use with that app (for example the Google account used to sign in).
  • A brief description of your request (e.g., "Please delete my account").

We may need to verify that the request concerns your account. Uninstalling an app does not necessarily delete a server-side account or purchase record. What is deleted and retained is described in the app's section of our Privacy Policy.

Email for Account Deletion

General Inquiries

For questions about a Nevitech app, its features or Nevitech, include the app name, your question and, if you want a reply, your contact details.

Email for General Inquiries

Technical Support

For technical issues, include the app name, app version, device model, operating-system version, a description of the problem and the steps to reproduce it. Remove personal information from any screenshot before attaching it.

Email for Technical Support

Purchases

For purchase problems, include the app name, whether you bought through Apple or Google, the product name and the store order reference if you have it. Never send card numbers, passwords, verification codes or account tokens. Refund eligibility and payment handling follow the relevant store's policies.

Email for Purchase Support

Feedback
