Reporting Claims of Copyright and/or Related Rights Infringement

We take claims of infringement of copyright and related rights seriously. We will respond to notices of alleged infringement that comply with applicable laws, rules and regulations. If you believe any material accessible on or from our Services infringes your copyright and/or related rights, you may request its removal by submitting a written notification (the "Copyright Notice") that includes substantially the following:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the copyrighted works or other protected subject matter you believe has been infringed or, if the claim involves multiple works, a representative list of them.
  • Identification of the material you believe is infringing, in a sufficiently precise manner to allow us to locate it, including the app or website and the electronic location of the material.
  • The name of the owner or legal representative and contact means to receive notifications.
  • Adequate information by which we can contact you (including your name, postal address, telephone number and, if available, email address).
  • A statement that, based on reasonable evidence, you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright and/or related rights owner.

Counter Notification Procedures

If you believe that material you posted was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter notification (a "Counter Notice") by email to nevitechoz@gmail.com. A Counter Notice must substantially include:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the material that was removed or disabled and the location where it appeared before removal.
  • Adequate information by which we can contact you (including your name, postal address, telephone number and, if available, email address).
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  • Supporting information evidencing your ownership or authorization for that use, or supporting its use under the limitations and exceptions to the rights protected by applicable intellectual property law.

If we receive a Counter Notice that meets these requirements, we will report it to the person who submitted the original notice and re-enable the content unless that person brings a judicial or administrative proceeding, a criminal complaint or an alternative dispute process within 15 business days of our report.

We are not obliged to supervise or monitor our users' systems or networks to actively search for possible infringements occurring online.

If you knowingly materially misrepresent that material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may be held liable for damages (including costs and attorneys' fees) under applicable law.

Repeat Infringers

It is our policy in appropriate circumstances to disable and/or terminate the accounts of users who are determined to be repeat infringers.

A repeat infringer is generally a user who has received multiple valid copyright infringement notices within a certain period, as determined at our discretion. We consider factors including the number and nature of notices and any counter notifications.

We also reserve the right to act in cases of serious copyright violations or fraudulent activity, even if a user does not strictly meet the repeat infringer threshold.
